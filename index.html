Full detail by job family

Title/family benchmark detail uses the 1,615 defensibly mapped compensation records. The full 2,103 records remain included in source and pay-band summaries. 10th–90th percentile (light) and 25th–75th (dark), with the median marked. Families under 5 records show only a thin avg/min/max marker, flagged low sample.

Figures are annualized base pay estimates. Payroll data was cleaned and annualized from each participating employer's own pay convention; self-reported survey pay was parsed from free text with outliers excluded; anonymized reported ranges reflect each participant's own "typical low–high" answer for a broader job group. Job-title QA update: title and job-family benchmarks now use only confirmed corrected mappings. Ambiguous titles in the manual-review queue are excluded rather than forced into an unrelated benchmark title.
